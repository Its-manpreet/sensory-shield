Sensory Shield

Softens flashing and loud sound, blurs what you choose. Everything stays on this device.

Screen access needed

To soften flashing in any app, Sensory Shield needs to see the screen. Frames are analysed in memory and never saved or sent anywhere.

  1. Open Privacy settings and turn on this app under Screen & System Audio Recording.
  2. Relaunch Sensory Shield.

Mix and match: each switch works on its own.

Child safety

On-device models look at pictures and read text when the screen changes, and blur what matches. Nothing is saved or sent.

Fears and phobias

    Higher blurs more readily, including some harmless things.

    Guardian (camera)

    If the camera sees strong, rhythmic movement for a while, Guardian asks "Are you OK?". If nobody answers, it sends a short text alert to the people you choose. Video stays on this device; only the alert text is sent.

    Not a medical device. It can miss seizures (for example absence or focal seizures, or when you are out of view) and can raise false alarms.
