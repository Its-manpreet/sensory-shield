Sensory Shield: trigger gallery

Each panel shows one kind of visual trigger. For sound, play any video with a sudden loud moment. Run Sensory Shield and watch it soften them. This page itself protects nothing.

Warning: some panels flash or flicker, which can trigger seizures or migraines. Every panel starts hidden. Warn anyone nearby before you start one.

Flashing

Black/white flashing at 6 per second. Expected: frosted glass within about a third of a second.

Hidden

Red / blue colour flicker

Red and blue swapping 5 times a second, with little brightness change. Expected: glass with the colour drained out.

Hidden

Sudden brightness

The whole page goes dark, then jumps to white. Expected: the white is eased in over about a second.

Constant motion

Shapes that never stop drifting. Expected: calmed only when "All constant motion" is on in settings.

Hidden
